Privacy

Your visit, your choice

The site works without analytics, accounts, forms, or advertising trackers.

Effective date: August 6, 2026

What we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, quiz answers, account information, or payment details on this website. Quiz scoring happens only in your browser.

Optional analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” Google Analytics 4 may receive limited information about page views, completed story reads, quiz completion scores, result-sharing actions, and outbound product clicks. Events never include quiz answers or information you type. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled.

Analytics does not load on localhost, Netlify previews, unapproved hostnames, or until you consent. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage.

Store purchases

Product browsing, checkout, payment, order support, and fulfillment occur on Printify. Printify’s privacy and commerce policies apply after you follow a store link.

Third-party links

Historical source links lead to institutions such as the National Archives, Library of Congress, National Park Service, and Smithsonian. Their own privacy policies apply.
