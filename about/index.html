About the project

Keep the story. Keep asking.

Spirit of 1776 pairs approachable history with original goods designed to start conversations—not end them.

Our mission

The American Revolution is often compressed into a handful of dates and famous names. We build short paths back into the evidence: what changed, what remained unresolved, and how later generations reshaped the meaning of revolutionary symbols.

Editorial standards

  • Prefer primary sources and public institutions over unsourced retellings.
  • Separate documented facts from tradition, interpretation, and uncertainty.
  • Represent the Revolution as a conflict experienced differently across communities.
  • Link directly to sources so families can investigate further.
  • Correct substantive errors openly and promptly.

Artwork standards

The launch collection uses original, digitally created illustration layers and deterministically rendered typography. It does not use the official America250 logo or claim official status. Generated concepts are reviewed for garbled text, invented affiliations, and inappropriate symbols before publication.

Independence statement

Spirit of 1776 is an independent educational and merchandise project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the United States Semiquincentennial Commission, America250, the National Archives, the Library of Congress, the National Park Service, or the Smithsonian Institution.
