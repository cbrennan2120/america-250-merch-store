About the project

A small project about big history.

Ten illustrated stories, a timeline, a quiz, and a made-to-order shop—all created to make American history easier to explore and more fun to share.

Hi, I’m Chris.

I’m Chris Brennan. I created Spirit of 1776 to make American history easier to explore and a little more fun to share. The stories follow the sources. The shop is where the chibi characters and history jokes get to loosen their collars.

What you’ll find here

Spirit of 1776 is a small independent project: ten illustrated stories, a timeline, a quiz, and a growing collection of made-to-order goods. I link the sources so you can check the history for yourself.

How I handle the history

  • Start with primary sources and public institutions whenever possible.
  • Separate documented facts from tradition, interpretation, and uncertainty.
  • Say when an illustration combines people or moments instead of recreating one documented scene.
  • Link directly to the evidence so you can keep reading.
  • Correct substantive errors openly and promptly.

How the artwork is made

The illustrations are developed with digital and AI-assisted tools, then reviewed, edited, typeset, and prepared for print by Spirit of 1776. Historical scenes are interpretive, and sources are linked with each story.
