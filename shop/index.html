How fulfillment works

Printed on demand

Printify produces items only after an order is submitted. Product pages will identify available sizes, colors, print provider, and estimated delivery.

Shipping at checkout

Compatible shipping options and costs appear during Printify checkout. Shipping is not included in the provisional prices shown here.

Returns and defects

Printify handles customer support. Damaged or defective products may qualify for a reprint or refund when reported with photo proof within 30 days. Because items are made to order, size or preference returns are generally not supported.
